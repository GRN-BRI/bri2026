# Accommodation

### Best Western Plus Jeonju Hotel

A block of 50 rooms has been reserved for GRN-BRI 2026 participants. 


- Distance: Approx. 15 min by taxi (5 km) from the venue.
- Address:41 Hyeonmu 1-gil, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si, Jeonbuk State, Republic of Korea
- Website: [http://www.bwplusjeonju.com](http://www.bwplusjeonju.com)

**Rates & Information**
- Standard Room: KRW 90,000 per room, per night
- Conference Discount：GRN-BRI 2026 participants receive a 10% discount on the room rate.
- Breakfast: KRW 19,000 per person
- Check-in / Check-out: 15:00 / 11:00

**How to Book**
- A dedicated reservation link for conference participants will be provided shortly.
- Room charges are payable directly to the hotel upon check-in.
- Please mention “GRN-BRI 2026” when making your reservation to receive the conference rate.
