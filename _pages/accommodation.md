# Accommodation

### Best Western Plus Jeonju Hotel (베스트웨스턴 플러스 전주호텔)

A block of 50 rooms has been reserved for GRN-BRI 2026 participants. 


- Distance: Approx. 15 min by taxi (5 km) from the venue.
- Address:41 Hyeonmu 1-gil, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si, Jeonbuk State, Republic of Korea
- Website: [http://www.bwplusjeonju.com](http://www.bwplusjeonju.com)

**Rates & Information**
- Standard Room: KRW 90,000 per room, per night
- Conference Discount：GRN-BRI 2026 participants receive a 10% discount on the room rate.
- Breakfast: KRW 19,000 per person
- Check-in / Check-out: 15:00 / 11:00

**How to Book**
- A dedicated reservation link for conference participants will be provided shortly.
- Room charges are payable directly to the hotel upon check-in.
- Please mention “GRN-BRI 2026” when making your reservation to receive the conference rate.

### Jeonju Wangyijimil Hanok Hotel (전주 왕의지밀 한옥호텔)

A premium traditional Korean hanok hotel, a short drive from Jeonju Hanok Village.

- Distance: Approx. 25 min by taxi (9 km) from the venue.
- Address: 5218-7 Chunhyang-ro, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si, Jeonbuk State, Republic of Korea
- Tel: +82-63-284-1004
- Website: [http://royalroom.co.kr](http://royalroom.co.kr)

**Rates & Information**
- Standard Room (Double or Twin): Approx. KRW 100,000–180,000 per room, per night (varies by date and booking channel)
- Larger family rooms: up to approx. KRW 320,000 per room, per night
- Breakfast: Available at extra charge (please check with the hotel)
- Check-in / Check-out: 16:00 / 11:00
- Free parking

**How to Book**
- Please book directly through the hotel website. No conference discount applies.
