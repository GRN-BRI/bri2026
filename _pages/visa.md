# Visa

#### Invitation Letter
Participants who require a visa or an official invitation letter for institutional travel approval may request one from the organizing committee after completing registration. Invitation letters cover the period of November 23–28, 2026 (including the pre-conference in Seoul).<br>
Requests: ([ahjo@jbnu.ac.kr](mailto:ahjo@jbnu.ac.kr))<br>

Please check whether your country is eligible for K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) at: [https://www.k-eta.go.kr](https://www.k-eta.go.kr)
