# Transportation Guide

The conference venue is **The International Convention Center, Jeonbuk National University (JBNU)**, Jeonju.

<div markdown="1" style="border:2px solid #14213d; border-radius:8px; padding:12px 18px; margin:14px 0 20px;">
**Quick overview — how to get to Jeonju**

| From | Recommended route | Travel time | Fare (adult) |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Incheon Airport (ICN)** | Intercity bus → **Jeonju Intercity Bus Terminal** → taxi to venue (10 min) | about 3 h + 10 min | KRW 32,000 + taxi about KRW 6,000 |
| **Gimpo Airport (GMP)** | Daihan Limousine → **Jeonju Limousine Terminal** → taxi to venue (13 min) | about 3 h 10 min + 13 min | KRW 25,800 + taxi about KRW 8,000 |
| **Seoul (by train)** | KTX → **Jeonju Station** → taxi to venue (13 min) | about 1 h 50 min + 13 min | KRW 34,600 + taxi about KRW 8,000 |
</div>

# How to Book Bus Tickets

<div markdown="1" style="border:2px solid #c0392b; background:#fdf1ef; border-radius:8px; padding:12px 18px; margin:12px 0 18px;">
**⚠ Book your RETURN ticket (Jeonju → Incheon Airport) now.**<br>
On 16 November, some buses on Saturday 28 November already had only 5–9 of 28 seats left. The last bus from Jeonju Intercity Bus Terminal to Incheon Airport on 28 November leaves at **17:10**.
</div>

You can get a bus ticket in three ways: **(1)** book online in advance on [TxBus (English)](https://intercitybuse.tmoney.co.kr/otck/trmlInfEnty.do) — recommended, **(2)** buy at the bus ticket counter at Incheon Airport (you can buy your return ticket at the same time), or **(3)** buy your return ticket at Jeonju Intercity Bus Terminal right after you arrive. # Route Details

## From Incheon International Airport (ICN)

#### Option A. Intercity bus to Jeonju Intercity Bus Terminal (recommended — closest to the venue)

| Step | Mode | Travel time | Fare (adult) |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Incheon Airport → Jeonju Intercity Bus Terminal | Intercity bus | about 3 h (T1) / 3 h 25 min (T2) | KRW 32,000 (late-night KRW 34,000) |
| Jeonju Intercity Bus Terminal → Venue | Taxi | about 10 min (3.8 km) | about KRW 6,000 |

#### Option B. Daihan Limousine to Jeonju Limousine Terminal

| Step | Mode | Travel time | Fare (adult) |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Incheon Airport → Jeonju Limousine Terminal | Limousine bus | about 3 h 20 min–3 h 40 min (T1) / 3 h 45 min–4 h 05 min (T2) | KRW 29,800–34,000 (late-night KRW 32,800) |
| Jeonju Limousine Terminal → Venue | Taxi | about 13 min (6.6 km) | about KRW 8,000 |

#### Option C. AREX and KTX train (if you prefer trains or plan to stop in Seoul)

| Step | Mode | Travel time | Fare (adult) |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Incheon Airport → Seoul Station | AREX (Airport Railroad) | Express 43 min (T1) / 51 min (T2); all-stop about 1 h | Express KRW 13,000; all-stop KRW 4,750–5,350 |
| Seoul Station → Jeonju Station | KTX | about 1 h 50 min | KRW 34,600 |
| Jeonju Station → Venue | Taxi | about 13 min (5.7 km) | about KRW 8,000 |

**Note:** Only a few KTX trains run from Seoul Station to Jeonju each day. For more departures, take Subway Line 1 two stops from Seoul Station to **Yongsan Station**, where KTX trains to Jeonju run more often. Train tickets: online or at the station.
