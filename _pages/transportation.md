# How to Book Bus Tickets

<div style="border:2px solid #c0392b;background:#fdf1ef;border-radius:8px;padding:12px 18px;margin:12px 0 18px;">
<b>⚠ Book your RETURN ticket (Jeonju → Incheon Airport) now.</b><br>
On 16 November, some buses on Saturday 28 November already had only 5–9 of 28 seats left. The last bus from Jeonju Intercity Bus Terminal to Incheon Airport on 28 November leaves at <b>17:10</b>.
</div>

You can get a bus ticket in three ways: **(1)** book online in advance on [TxBus (English)](https://intercitybuse.tmoney.co.kr/otck/trmlInfEnty.do) — recommended, **(2)** buy at the bus ticket counter at Incheon Airport (you can buy your return ticket at the same time), or **(3)** buy your return ticket at Jeonju Intercity Bus Terminal right after you arrive. The guide below shows each step with screenshots and maps.
